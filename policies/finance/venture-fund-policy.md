# Venture Fund Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active — fund uncapitalized at launch  
**Edit authority:** CEO only

## Purpose

Fund approved revenue-generating venture experiments operated by the Venture Director under approved capability policies.

## Fund Structure

The Venture Fund is a standing fund maintained by the CEO. It is separate from the CEO's personal finances in all accounting, tracking, and decision-making.

- **Current balance:** $0 (uncapitalized at launch)
- **Capitalization authority:** CEO only
- **Initial capitalization:** Venture Director proposes an amount once venture candidates are identified; CEO approves. No autonomous funding.
- **Replenishment:** CEO only, on explicit approval. No automatic replenishment.

## Per-Venture Allocation Rules

- Each venture receives a CEO-approved budget allocation at Stage 3 (CEO Approval gate)
- Per-venture budget is a hard ceiling — the venture terminates if the ceiling is reached without CEO approval to continue
- The Venture Director must escalate at 80% of any per-venture budget before the limit is reached
- Physical accounts may be shared across ventures; isolation is enforced by policy-level per-venture tracking in the portfolio ledger, not physical account separation

## Maximum Acceptable Loss

Every active venture must define a Maximum Acceptable Loss (MAL) before receiving any funding. MAL is the worst financially and reputationally acceptable outcome if the venture fails completely.

- MAL is proposed by the Venture Director and approved by the CEO at Stage 3
- The sum of all active venture MALs must not exceed the current Venture Fund balance
- If aggregate active MALs approach the fund balance, the Venture Director must escalate to the President Agent immediately

## Prohibited Activities (No Exceptions)

- Securities trading of any kind
- Margin, leverage, options, futures, short selling, or any instrument capable of losses exceeding invested capital
- Debt issuance or brokerage activity
- Legal obligations, contracts, or binding agreements without explicit CEO approval
- Hiring humans, freelancers, or external contractors
- Forming legal entities or opening financial accounts
- Managing the CEO's personal investments

## Permitted Activities

Venture spending may cover only activities within the Venture Director's approved capability (`capabilities/venture-operations.md`) and the specific CEO-approved scope for each venture. All spending executes through the relevant operational director — the Venture Director holds no funds directly.

## Pending CEO Decisions

The following parameters are not yet set and must be established by CEO input before ventures can be capitalized:

- Venture Fund initial capitalization amount
- Individual venture budget ceiling (default cap absent specific approval)
- Maximum aggregate MAL as a fraction of fund balance
- Approved venture wallet provider and structure

These items do not block the Venture Director from Stage 1 (Discovery) and Stage 2 (Proposal) work. They must be resolved before Stage 3 (CEO Approval) can be completed for any venture.
