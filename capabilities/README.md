# Capabilities

Capability policies for all active organizational capabilities. CEO edit only.

A capability policy is the source of execution authority. It specifies which roles may act, on which systems, at what authority level, under what conditions, and subject to what spending and loss limits. A role has no authority to execute beyond what its capability policies explicitly grant.
