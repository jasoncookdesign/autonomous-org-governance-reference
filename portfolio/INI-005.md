## Outcome

Policy gaps are tracked systematically rather than patched reactively. The CEO processes governance changes with context and deliberation rather than under operational pressure. The President Agent has a backlog to manage rather than a stream of ad hoc escalations.

## Scope

**Deliverables:**
1. `governance/policy-gap-register.md` — the register itself, with initial entries seeded from gaps observed during the 2026-06-05 session
2. `templates/policy-gap-entry-template.md` — standardized entry format
3. **Governance Manual amendment** — adds § Policy Gap Management with workflow policy (draft already exists in governance evaluation document)

This is a lightweight initiative. The register is a markdown file; no tooling, no infrastructure.

**Initial gaps to seed:**
- Near-miss tracking not yet added to incident log format
- Incident log format not yet standardized across all directors
- Director authority ceilings not yet defined for all directors (some have capability docs, some do not)
