# Venture Evaluation Framework (Draft — Pending CEO Approval)

**Status:** Draft — not approved  
**Prepared by:** Governance repository initial build  
**Edit authority:** CEO only  
**Note:** Per `roles/venture-director.md`, the Venture Director must develop a formal scoring framework and submit it to the CEO for approval via the policy change process. Until approved, all venture proposals must include a qualitative risk/opportunity assessment. This draft is a starting point for that proposal — it has no policy authority until the CEO approves it.

---

## Proposed Scoring Dimensions

Score each opportunity on a 1–5 scale per dimension. Higher scores are more favorable.

| Dimension | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Acceptable) | 5 (Strong) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Startup cost** | High upfront investment required | Moderate; manageable within fund | Low; minimal capital to test |
| **Automation potential** | Requires heavy manual operation | Partially automatable | Fully or near-fully automatable by org |
| **Revenue potential** | Marginal or speculative | Meaningful if successful | High ceiling with clear path |
| **Competitive intensity** | Saturated; entrenched players | Competitive but accessible | Low competition or clear differentiator |
| **Maintenance burden** | High ongoing operational demand | Moderate | Low; self-sustaining once launched |
| **Regulatory risk** | Significant compliance exposure | Some considerations; manageable | Minimal regulatory surface |
| **Org capability alignment** | Requires capabilities the org lacks | Partial fit | Directly within existing org capabilities |

## Proposed Scoring Interpretation

| Total Score | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 28–35 | Strong candidate — prioritize for proposal |
| 20–27 | Viable — proceed with qualitative caveats |
| 14–19 | Marginal — proceed only with strong qualitative case |
| 7–13 | Weak — do not advance without CEO direction |

## Proposed Minimum Threshold

Ventures scoring below 14 should not advance to Stage 2 (Proposal) without explicit CEO direction.
