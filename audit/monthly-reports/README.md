# Monthly Reports

Archive of weekly governance summaries and monthly capability audit reports delivered to the CEO. Filed here after delivery for reference and audit trail.

## Filing Convention

Weekly governance summaries: `YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-summary.md`  
Monthly capability audits: `YYYY-MM-capability-audit.md`

## Retention

Reports are retained indefinitely as part of the organizational audit trail. They contain operational metadata (Class B) and may contain references to Class C content. Full Class C content remains in the KB under normal retention rules.
