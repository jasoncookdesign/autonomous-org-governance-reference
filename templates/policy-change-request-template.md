# Policy Change Request Template

**Version:** 1.0  
**Usage:** Use this format when proposing a policy change to the CEO. Agents may propose; only the CEO may approve, publish, or activate. File as a draft in the policy change proposal area and deliver to CEO via email.

---

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]  
**Proposed by:** [Role name]  
**Via:** [President Agent]  
**Subject:** Policy Change Proposal — [Brief description]

---

## Operational Problem

[What situation, gap, or friction in current operations prompted this proposal? Be specific. Reference actual incidents or escalations where applicable.]

## Current Policy Limitation

[Which policy document and section is insufficient or missing? Cite by file path and section. Quote the relevant text if helpful.]

## Proposed Change

[Exact proposed language, or a clear description of the change. Be precise enough that the CEO could approve it without asking clarifying questions.]

## Risk Created by Change

[What new risks would this change introduce? Be honest — proposals that omit risks are less trustworthy.]

## Risk Reduced by Change

[What current risk or friction does this change address?]

## Recommended Authority Level

[If this involves a new capability or authority expansion: what authority level is proposed? Why is that level appropriate rather than a lower one?]

## Recommended Maximum Acceptable Loss

[If this involves a new capability or spending authority: what is the proposed MAL? How was it determined?]

## Dependencies

[Does this change require other policy changes to function correctly? List them.]

---

*This is a proposal only. It is not active policy. The proposing agent must not behave as if this change has been approved until the CEO explicitly approves and publishes it.*
